How to use the HipoBuy spreadsheet

1. Open the exact listing

Open a product on CAFANS, select Buy Link and choose HipoBuy when it appears. Compare the product title, color and selected size with the source listing.

2. Check fit and QC photos

Compare the seller’s measurements with an item that fits you. When warehouse photos are available, check the size tag, color, quantity and visible condition before approving dispatch.

3. Compare the total cost

Record item price, domestic delivery, agent fees and the international shipping quote separately. Compare routes using the destination and quoted billable weight. Check whether taxes or other charges are included.

Frequently asked questions

What is a HipoBuy spreadsheet?

It is a product research list that groups listings by category and gives you a way to compare options before using HipoBuy as a shopping agent. This page links to the CAFANS product listings.

Can I keep my own shortlist?

Filter the catalog and download the visible rows as a CSV. Open the file in Excel or Google Sheets, then add columns for color, size, the current quote and the date you checked it.

Where can I see the latest price and availability?

Open the CAFANS product page and check the exact variant at the seller or agent. Confirm the current price, stock and any return conditions before paying.

What should I do if the HipoBuy option is missing?

Look for the original seller listing on CAFANS. Check with HipoBuy whether it currently accepts that listing and variant before placing an order.
